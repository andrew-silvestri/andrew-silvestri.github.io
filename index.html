Energy

Techno-economics

Industrial heat electrification break-even

Cost of heat from an electric boiler against a gas boiler in Texas. The gap between the fuel prices decides the outcome. Break-even electricity is about 1.5 cents per kilowatt hour at $3.50 gas, and capital cost moves it very little.

Optimisation

Battery revenue simulator

Linear program over 8,760 hours. Value flattens above four hours of storage duration, which is the expected result, now measured.

Chemical

Chemical engineering

Why our sorbent could not do direct air capture

Using the laboratory's own isotherm parameters, the material holds almost nothing at 420 parts per million. A negative result, and the reason the project changed material.

Industry tooling

Pipeline liquid holdup calculator

A spreadsheet with 19,653 simulation runs, ported to an interactive tool. The port matched the workbook formulas exactly, and showed that the workbook's displayed values carried a stale calculation.

Climate research

Life-cycle · interactive

The true climate cost

Twenty products taken apart stage by stage, with the allocation on every edge and the lifetime of every vehicle exposed rather than buried. Change the route and it recomputes instead of looking up an answer somebody prepared.

Agriculture · synthesis

Nitrogen-fixing beans

A legume makes its own fertiliser; a cereal needs a Haber–Bosch plant to make it instead. Four routes are being engineered to move that ability, and they are at wildly different stages — one is routine, one produces structures that do not work, one has never produced a plant.

Materials · interactive

What a running shoe is made of

Four constructions in three dimensions, every layer named by its polymer. Six or seven plastics, bonded to each other, worn out in months: the recycling problem is in the construction rather than the disposal.

Other work

Sonification · interactive

Skylines, played

A skyline and a spectrum analyser are the same picture, so the picture is played. Twenty-seven cities built from real building heights and coordinates; turn on the spot and the sound turns with you. What is measured, what is assumed and what is invented are each labelled.

Allometry · interactive

Longevity quotient

How long an animal lives, against how long an animal of its size should live. Body mass predicts lifespan across fourteen orders of magnitude; the quotient is what is left once that relationship is divided out.

Evidence · interactive

What a hobby is worth

A hobby finder that ranks by how good the evidence is rather than how good the claim sounds. Causal designs, cohorts and bare correlations are kept apart, the confounders are named, and the popular thing that does not work is named too.

Desktop · interactive

The bookshelf

A wallpaper built from a Goodreads export: your books as drawn spines, or as a wall of their real covers brought to one finish. The CSV is parsed in the browser and never leaves it.

Built with Python, Julia and public data. All source is downloadable.

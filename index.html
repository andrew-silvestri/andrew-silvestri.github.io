Gas

Distribution · rates

Lost gas

Customers pay for the gas their utility reports as lost, and for a typical system the figure is smaller than the spread meter error alone produces.

Capacity · interactive

Pipeline capacity

Each new pipeline out of the Permian buys the basin only a year or so; when the pipes are full, the price of gas at the Waha hub falls below zero and producers pay to have it taken away.

Bonds · rates

Storm-cost bonds

Customers of ten Texas and Oklahoma gas utilities are still repaying Winter Storm Uri's gas costs through their bills, until as late as 2047; over a third of it is interest.

Processing · archived

Ethane rejection

With too few plastics plants and export ships to take it, up to 27 percent of the ethane in American gas in 2014, a raw material for plastic, was burned as fuel.

Power

EIA and ERCOT prices · since 2001

Electric boiler break-even

At the price Texas industry pays for electricity, an electric boiler has not beaten a gas one in any month since 2001.

ERCOT prices · since 2011

Battery revenue

A big battery in Texas earns by buying power cheap and selling it dear, and on ERCOT's prices since 2011 a handful of scarcity days decide its year.

Climate

Life-cycle model · interactive

Food footprints

A kilogram of beef from cleared Brazilian pasture emits as much greenhouse gas as a long drive; what a food is and how it was grown decide its footprint, and shipping matters little unless it flies.

AnAge, Amniote and four more databases · 7,873 species

Longevity quotient

Bats and primates outlive their size by one and a half to two and a half times and pheasants and their kin about half, by a margin that depends on how briefly some animals were watched.

OpenAlex and the Red List · 9,113 birds

Endangered-bird research

Count research alone, which is less than a third of what a literature search returns, and the typical critically endangered bird draws about five times the work of a comparable common one.

Wikidata and OpenStreetMap · sonification

Skyline sonification

By its own records, one tall building in twenty that an open database lists for big cities is demolished or never finished; the towers that stand are played as sound.

Geodesy · interactive

Plate motion

Plate interiors hold their shape to about half a millimetre a year, a precision that says almost nothing about where they will be.

Labor Department and BLS · 1950 to 2025

Unemployment insurance

In an average week of 2025, about 26 of every 100 unemployed Americans were claiming unemployment insurance, about half the share of the 1950s, and in Florida about 8.

PNAS estimate, rebuilt · Bureau of Mines · NHANES

Leaded gasoline

Americans born in the late 1960s lost about six IQ points each, on average, to lead in early childhood; the choice of dose-response curve alone moves the national total by a factor of five.

EIA, USGS, EPA, USDA · policy chain

Oxygenated gasoline

A 1990 rule to put oxygen in city gasoline led to MTBE in groundwater, bans or limits in 25 states and, through the law that ended it, corn ethanol in about one gallon in ten of American gasoline.

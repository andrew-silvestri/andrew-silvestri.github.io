Lost gas

Customers pay for the gas their utility reports as lost, and for a typical system the figure is smaller than the spread meter error alone produces.

Capacity · interactive

Pipeline capacity

Each new pipeline out of the Permian buys the basin only a year or so; when the pipes are full, the price of gas at the Waha hub falls below zero and producers pay to have it taken away.

Bonds · rates

Storm-cost bonds

Customers of ten Texas and Oklahoma gas utilities are still repaying Winter Storm Uri's gas costs through their bills, until as late as 2047; over a third of it is interest.

Processing · archived

Ethane rejection

With too few plastics plants and export ships to take it, up to 27 percent of the ethane in American gas in 2014, a raw material for plastic, was burned as fuel.

Electric boiler break-even

At the price Texas industry pays for electricity, an electric boiler has not beaten a gas one in any month since 2001.

ERCOT prices · since 2011

Battery revenue

A big battery in Texas earns by buying power cheap and selling it dear, and on ERCOT's prices since 2011 a handful of scarcity days decide its year.

Climate

Life-cycle model · interactive

Food footprints

A kilogram of beef from cleared Brazilian pasture emits as much greenhouse gas as a long drive; what a food is and how it was grown decide its footprint, and shipping matters little unless it flies.

GCB · Ocean model · Published studies

Algae carbon

Ocean algae fix several times more carbon than fossil fuels emit, and almost none of it stays out of the air for a century.

Field trials · evidence review

Climate messaging

Pre-selecting a green electricity tariff raised its share of sales from 7 to 69 percent in one trial, while carbon labels, norm signs and better dish names each moved meal choices by under two percentage points.

Eurobarometer 2025 · review of published savings

Climate effort

The three climate actions Europeans name most often save about a seventh of a tonne of greenhouse gas a year each, and the three they name least often over a tonne.

Life-cycle model · digital prototype

Running-shoe footprint

Most attempts to green a running shoe start with its plastic, but in a model built from public data the factories' energy weighs as much, and a redesign alone cuts about a fifth.

Science

AnAge, Amniote and four more databases · 7,873 species

Longevity quotient

Bats and primates outlive their size by one and a half to two and a half times and pheasants and their kin about half, by a margin that depends on how briefly some animals were watched.

OpenAlex and the Red List · 9,113 birds

Endangered-bird research

Count research alone, which is less than a third of what a literature search returns, and the typical critically endangered bird draws about five times the work of a comparable common one.

Geodesy · interactive

Plate motion

Plate interiors hold their shape to about half a millimetre a year, a precision that says almost nothing about where they will be.

Hatton 2019, Eker 2018, OWID, BEA · one axis

Scales

Per kilogram, a resting human body gives off thousands of times more power than the Sun, and living things from bacteria to elephants share a narrow band that a single law of size cannot explain.

Fossils, molecular clocks, SETI surveys · five steps

The Great Filter

Of the five big steps that took Earth from chemistry to radio, three happened only once, and the empty sky cannot yet say whether the hardest step is behind humanity or ahead.

ASHRAE comfort votes, MARHYS vent fluids · biophysics

The gradient of life

Every cell holds back a voltage across a membrane 5 nanometres thick; nerves pay to keep theirs ready, and 86,000 comfort votes show the pull of a set point.

52 dated cases · idea to decisive event

Philosophers’ guesses about nature

Right guesses about nature from antiquity waited a median of 2,088 years for the measurement that confirmed them; after 1400, wrong ideas were refuted faster than right ones were confirmed.

Society & Health

Labor Department and BLS · 1950 to 2025

Unemployment insurance

In an average week of 2025, about 26 of every 100 unemployed Americans were claiming unemployment insurance, about half the share of the 1950s, and in Florida about 8.

PNAS estimate, rebuilt · Bureau of Mines · NHANES

Leaded gasoline

Americans born in the late 1960s lost about six IQ points each, on average, to lead in early childhood; the choice of dose-response curve alone moves the national total by a factor of five.

EIA, USGS, EPA, USDA · policy chain

Oxygenated gasoline

A 1990 rule to put oxygen in city gasoline led to MTBE in groundwater, bans or limits in 25 states and, through the law that ended it, corn ethanol in about one gallon in ten of American gasoline.

NASA, McDowell, JPL Horizons · evidence map

Depression in space

Every validated measurement of mood in flight comes from Earth orbit and about 30 people, while one four-person Mars mission would log about 13 times all human time spent beyond it.

BLS American Time Use Survey · 24-hour clock

Night work

Government time diaries show America's 5.1 million night workers sleeping in broken blocks, at hours when two in three people are awake, and through half the evening.

Berlin Marathon results · 1999 to 2025

The sub-3 marathon

Of the Berlin runners who passed halfway on schedule for a three-hour marathon, about one in three finished under three hours; the rest lost their time after 25 km.

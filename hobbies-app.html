What a hobby is worth

Describe your week and this ranks activities by what the published evidence would predict, and says how good that evidence is. It is not a prediction about you: nearly all of it is population-average, and most of it is observational. The two bars are kept separate on purpose.

Reading it

Evidence is how well the effect is established, not how large it is. Randomised trials and Mendelian randomisation sit at the top because they survive the objection that healthier people simply take up more hobbies.

Headroom is how much room your stated week leaves for that particular change. Someone already meeting the activity guidelines has little headroom on exercise and may have a great deal on company.

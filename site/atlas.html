The atlas

Seven layers of one model. 7,192 nodes, each on a real coordinate.

Open the atlas full screen

The atlas runs entirely in your browser. Choose a scenario and press Run. The side panel reports how many nodes changed, how many steps the model needed to settle, and the largest single effect. The counts on the tabs show where the change landed in the other layers. Click any node to read its data source, then apply your own change there.

The seven layers

The world layers use a Mercator projection of the country polygon set. The climate layer draws the measured series. The behaviour layer is a labelled schematic, not a medical image.

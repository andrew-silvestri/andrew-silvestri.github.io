What is real here

Real: titles, authors, page counts, years and ratings from your export, and in cover mode the covers themselves, each fitted to a spine-like proportion and given one shared finish. Chosen: the drawn spines — a design keyed to each book, its width from its page count, its dress from its era. Assumed, and counted in the caption: a book with no page count in the export is drawn at your library’s median width, and a book with no year is dressed plain. No database of photographed book spines exists, for any price, so this page does not pretend to one.
